
# Acme Cloud Refund Policy

## Subscription Refunds

Customers may request a refund for a subscription within 30 days
of the original purchase date.

Refund requests must include the account email address and
the transaction ID.

## Processing Time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.
Processing usually takes 5–7 business days.

## Duplicate Charges

If a customer was charged twice for the same subscription,
the support team must investigate both transaction IDs.

Do not promise a refund until the duplicate charge has been verified.

## Exceptions

This policy does not automatically approve refund requests.
The support team must verify the purchase date, transaction
details, and applicable subscription terms.
